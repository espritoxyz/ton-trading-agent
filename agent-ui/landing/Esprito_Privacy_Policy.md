## 4. Information We Collect

### 4.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Wallet Address:** Your public blockchain wallet address, which you connect to the Platform to use the Services. This is a pseudonymous identifier on the Blockchain;
- **Telegram Account Data:** If you access the Platform through the Telegram Mini App, we may receive your Telegram user ID, username, first name, last name, language preference, and profile photo (as authorized by Telegram's Mini App API);
- **Trading Commands and Communications:** The text commands, queries, and instructions you submit to the AI Agent through the Platform's natural language interface;
- **Subscription and Payment Information:** If you subscribe to a paid tier, we collect information necessary to process your payment, which may include your chosen subscription plan and payment transaction details. We do not directly store full payment card details; payment processing is handled by third-party payment processors;
- **Support Communications:** Information you provide when contacting our support team, including your messages, email address (if provided), and any attachments;
- **Feedback and Survey Data:** Any feedback, suggestions, survey responses, or other voluntary information you provide to us.

### 4.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device and Browser Information:** Device type, operating system, browser type and version, screen resolution, device identifiers, and language settings;
- **Usage Data:** Pages visited, features used, commands entered (in anonymized/aggregated form), frequency and duration of use, click patterns, and navigation paths;
- **Log Data:** IP address, access timestamps, referring URLs, error logs, and server response codes;
- **Location Data:** Approximate geographic location derived from your IP address (country/region level only; we do not collect precise GPS location);
- **Performance Data:** Platform load times, latency metrics, error rates, and other technical performance indicators.

### 4.3 Blockchain Data (Publicly Available)

When you execute transactions through the Platform, the following data is recorded on the public Blockchain and is inherently visible to anyone: your wallet address, transaction hashes, token amounts, counterparty addresses (e.g., DEX smart contract addresses), timestamps, and Gas Fees paid. This data is not collected by Esprito per se — it is created on and retrieved from the public Blockchain as a necessary consequence of executing transactions. Esprito may index and store copies of this publicly available Blockchain data to provide Services (e.g., transaction history, portfolio analytics).

### 4.4 Information from Third Parties

- **Blockchain Analytics Providers:** We may use third-party blockchain analytics services (e.g., indexers, APIs) to enrich transaction data, detect fraud, and comply with applicable laws;
- **Telegram Platform:** Data shared through Telegram's Mini App platform in accordance with Telegram's terms and privacy practices;
- **Market Data Providers:** Price feeds, trading volume data, and other market information from third-party data sources (this is generally non-personal data).

## 9. Blockchain Data and On-Chain Transparency

You acknowledge and understand that the nature of public Blockchain technology means that certain data associated with your use of the Services — including your wallet address, transaction details, token balances, and interactions with smart contracts — is publicly accessible on the Blockchain. This information is not "collected" or "controlled" by Esprito in the traditional sense; it is a permanent, immutable record on a decentralized network.

While wallet addresses are pseudonymous (they do not directly reveal your real-world identity), it may be possible for third parties to link your wallet address to your identity through Blockchain analytics, public disclosures, or other means. Esprito is not responsible for any de-anonymization that may occur through on-chain analysis by third parties.

Due to the immutable nature of Blockchain technology, Esprito cannot fulfill requests to delete, modify, or restrict access to data that has been recorded on the Blockchain. The rights described in Section 15 apply only to data within Esprito's control (i.e., off-chain data stored on our servers).

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws (including the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and other applicable legislation), you may have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

**Right of Access:** You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and information about how we process it.

**Right to Rectification:** You have the right to request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

**Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data, subject to certain exceptions (e.g., legal compliance obligations). Please note that this right does not extend to data recorded on the Blockchain, which is immutable.

**Right to Restriction of Processing:** You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

**Right to Data Portability:** You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format, and to transmit it to another controller.

**Right to Object:** You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data based on our legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.

**Right to Withdraw Consent:** Where processing is based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

**Right Not to Be Subject to Automated Decision-Making:** You have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, which produces legal effects or similarly significant effects, except as permitted by applicable law.

**California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA):** If you are a California resident, you have additional rights including the right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used, the right to delete personal information, the right to opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell personal information), and the right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

We will respond to your request within the timeframe required by applicable law (typically within 30 days, or as otherwise required). We may need to verify your identity before processing your request. If your request is manifestly unfounded or excessive, we may charge a reasonable fee or decline to act on the request, as permitted by law.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority if you believe that our processing of your personal data violates applicable data protection laws.
